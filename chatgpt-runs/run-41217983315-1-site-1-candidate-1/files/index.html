Josh Brooks Construction and Renovation Inc. is a Longmont-based general contractor founded in 2014. Owner Josh Brooks brings 30 years of construction experience to residential roofing, remodeling and restoration projects.

Every project is run start to finish by the company as your general contractor. The company does not take subcontract work for other general contractors or owner-managed projects.
