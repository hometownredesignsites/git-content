# Visual Effects and Interaction Standards

Use purposeful polish that supports clarity and conversion: refined typography and spacing; responsive layouts; intentional image treatment; subtle transitions; CTA hover/focus/active feedback; navigation transitions; progressive scroll reveals; and restrained card/image hover effects where useful.

- Effects must not cause layout shifts, obscure content, or depend solely on hover.
- Keep keyboard focus visible and interactions accessible.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; essential content must remain visible and functional without animation.
- Use progressive enhancement: content should be available if JavaScript or motion effects fail.
- Preserve source photography, order, full-bleed hero, and original useful components as required by the skill.
- Do not use motion or effects to mask missing content or poor visual hierarchy.
