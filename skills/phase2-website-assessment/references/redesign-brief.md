# Redesign Brief

- Inspect the assigned live source website; confirm factual business details, services, locations, contact details, brand cues and source imagery.
- Identify current UX/design weaknesses and opportunities without unsupported claims.
- Preserve source facts, sections, working links, contact details, forms and form fields/options, useful interactions, and all source image `src`/`srcset` values and ordering unless a demonstrable defect or explicit authorization justifies a change.
- Make a premium, clearly differentiated responsive redesign. Avoid generic template layouts, filler, repetitive cards, empty slogans, and unsupported content.
- Preserve valid full-bleed heroes; improve contrast, crop, focal point and responsive behavior rather than breaking immersion.
- Keep conversion paths intact. Disclose demo forms with no backend; don't transmit or store form data or imply successful delivery.
- Before return, compare against original source and confirm hierarchy, image treatment, typography, spacing, responsiveness, interaction, content completeness, and CTA clarity have improved.
