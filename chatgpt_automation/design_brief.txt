Derive a distinctive visual system from the source site's visible identity and audience. Prioritize its actual logo, colors, photography, language, and motifs; refresh them thoughtfully rather than copying the existing layout. Use a location only when the source confirms it and it informs the audience or design.

Set an intentional palette, type pairing, spacing, image treatment, and one or two relevant motifs. Build a clear hierarchy and purposeful section composition around the organization's real conversion path. Match images to their subjects. Avoid filler sections, generic gradients, repeated card grids, and decorative complexity without a usability purpose.

Design mobile deliberately: adapt navigation, image crops, content order, spacing, and CTA prominence. Keep the result readable, keyboard usable, and accessible at all viewport sizes. Use an HTML5 UP template only as structural inspiration when a named template genuinely fits; otherwise choose an original direction.
